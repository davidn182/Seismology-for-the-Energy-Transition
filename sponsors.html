Sponsors

Support the next generation of monitoring seismologists

SET 2027 is free for all participants. Sponsorship covers coffee, lunches, the icebreaker and travel grants for participants from countries where monitoring programmes are still developing.

What sponsors receive

  • Your logo on this website and in the workshop materials
  • A stand during coffee breaks and poster sessions
  • Contact with 20–30 early-career seismologists working on geothermal and geological storage

Packages are flexible. Contact us to discuss what fits your organisation.
