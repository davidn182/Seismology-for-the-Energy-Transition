Workshop on seismic monitoring tools for geothermal energy and geological storage

Three days of hands-on training for early-career seismologists working on geothermal doublets, CO₂ storage and underground hydrogen storage. Learn to build earthquake catalogs, image the subsurface and design monitoring networks, using open data. Attendance is free. Participants who submit an abstract get priority.

Why this workshop

Geothermal doublets and storage sites for CO₂ and hydrogen are being commissioned across Europe at pace. They all inject, and sometimes withdraw, fluids from a reservoir, which can change the stress on nearby faults. Often the only early warning is microseismicity.

Interpreting it is a measurement problem: detecting small events in noisy records, locating them well enough to link them to a process, and separating operational from background seismicity. That needs three things, one per day of this workshop: a baseline catalog, an accurate velocity model, and a well-designed network.

Who should come

PhD candidates, postdocs and early-career professionals at agencies and companies monitoring geothermal, CCS or hydrogen storage sites. Mixed experience levels are welcome.

We especially encourage applicants from countries where monitoring programmes for these operations are still developing. A small number of travel grants are available.
