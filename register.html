Register & abstracts

Submit an abstract to secure your place

Attendance is free and places are limited. All participants are invited to submit an abstract, and those who do are given priority. Remaining places are allocated to attend-only registrations.

How it works

  1. Register using the form below. Choose “abstract” or “attend only”.
  2. Abstract submitters include a title and an abstract of up to 300 words, and say whether they prefer a talk or a poster.
  3. The committee reviews abstracts and confirms places by email. Attend-only registrations are confirmed after the abstract review, in order of registration.
  4. Two weeks before the workshop we send the datasets and set-up instructions for the hands-on sessions.

Topics

We welcome abstracts on seismic monitoring of geothermal energy, CO₂ storage, underground hydrogen storage and related subsurface operations, including:

  • Earthquake catalogs, completeness and statistics of induced seismicity
  • Velocity models and seismic imaging of reservoirs
  • Detection and location of microseismicity, network design
  • Distributed acoustic sensing, seismic interferometry and ambient noise
  • Case studies from operating sites

Travel grants

A limited number of travel grants are available, prioritising researchers from countries where seismic monitoring programmes for geological storage, including CCS and UHS, and geothermal energy are still developing. Tick the travel-grant box in the registration form and add a short statement of need. Selection criteria will be published before the deadline.
